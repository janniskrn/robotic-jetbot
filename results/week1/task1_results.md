# Task 1 results

Generated by `notebooks/mini_av/analyze_runs.py`; graphs in `usb/images/results/week1/`.

Modes: **baseline** = stock JetBot style (plain P steering, constant speed 0.32); **nocurve** = our steering
(PD, gain scheduling, feedforward off because no curve model) at the same constant speed; **adaptive** = our
steering plus curve-based speed and curvature feedforward from the curve model. Direction is measured from
the log (sign of the mean lane position). Run versions: `pd_proven` used KD 0.03 (D4c); `adaptive_ccw_5`
had no motor watchdog yet; `adaptive_cw_7` is the final configuration. `lane lost steps` counts steps with
lane visible below 0.5 (the run stops after 0.5 s without the lane).

Summary: the baseline lost the lane after about 20 s (2 of 2 runs). Our steering drove 60 s clockwise at
constant speed but lost the lane in the sharp counterclockwise curve (10.9 s) without feedforward. With the
curve model (adaptive), the robot drove 60 s in both directions, faster on straights (about 0.38) and slower
in curves (0.34-0.36).
