The swerve now rejoins nearer the middle of the lane

Track run 03-22-31, 5 swerves on the straight: after the curve the robot turned in just past the first line and drove on along the lane's border. Fix: 5 cm straight on before the turn in (ARC2).

The curve's end mark no longer ends the phase: the robot drives straight on until 0.2 s after the border crossing (lines 363 to 365; after run 04-04-01 counted from the crossing, ARC4). Line 341 then ends the phase and the turn in begins.

In a curve the robot now always passes on the inside

Run 04-21-11: the first two cups stood in a right curve. The driving direction was not known yet, so the robot passed left, on the outside, and the second swerve ended in line recovery. Fix: while the robot brakes, the curve model picks the inside of the curve, both ways round (PASS3).

Around a cup in a curve, the robot now finds the road again

Runs 03-23-36 and 03-24-51: the curve around the cup had to last 3 s, a rule made for straights (ARC1). In a curve the road bends back toward the robot, so it crossed the whole road and lost it; in 03-24-51 the lane was seen, sure and centered, 0.5 to 1.9 s into the curve and ignored. Fix: with the cup in a curve, the earlier marks end the curve (ARC3).
